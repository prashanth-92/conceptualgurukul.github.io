On conceptual teaching

In class

A chapter, start to finish.

The same four moves every week, for every topic — whether it is polynomials in class 9 or integration in class 12.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Begin with the question the concept exists to answer, in plain language.

  2. 02

    Derivation

    Build the formula on the board together, one line at a time.

  3. 03

    Practice

    Worked examples first, then variations solved without help.

  4. 04

    Board answers

    Write it the way CBSE marks it — steps, notation and the final line.

FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Which classes and subjects are taught?

Classes 9 and 10: CBSE Maths and Science. Classes 11 and 12: CBSE Maths only.

Is this only for CBSE students?

Yes. Teaching follows the CBSE syllabus and the way CBSE marks answers.

Is Science taught for classes 11 and 12?

No. For classes 11 and 12, only Mathematics is taught.

Do you offer counselling and career guidance?

Yes. Alongside tuition, the centre offers educational counselling on subjects and streams, and career guidance for what comes after class 12. Mention it in your enquiry and you’ll get the details that apply.

Where exactly is the centre?

In West Mambalam, Chennai. The exact address and directions are shared when you enquire, so you can check the route before visiting.

What are the timings and fees?

Current timings and fees are shared on enquiry. Mention the class and a preferred slot, and you’ll get the details that apply to that batch.

How do I join a batch?

Use the enquiry form or call. Mention the class and a preferred time, and you’ll get the current batch schedule and how to join.
